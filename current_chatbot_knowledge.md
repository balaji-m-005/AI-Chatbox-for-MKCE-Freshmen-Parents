## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q: What is MKCE? Tell me about your organization
**A:** MKCE stands for M. Kumarasamy College of Engineering. It is an autonomous engineering college located in Karur, Tamil Nadu. Established in 2000, it offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology. The college is affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE.

---

### Q: Who is the principal of MKCE?
**A:** Dr. B. S. Murugan, M.E., Ph.D., is the Principal and also a Professor in CSE.

---

### Q: Tell me about canteen facilities at MKCE
**A:** MKCE has a spacious and hygienic canteen facility that provides nutritious food to students and staff. The canteen serves breakfast, lunch, and snacks at affordable prices. It offers both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. The canteen is located near the main academic block and operates from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on all working days.
